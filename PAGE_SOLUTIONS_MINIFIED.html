Endpoint Security

Cyber adversaries’ efforts to bypass corporate defenses start usually by targeting endpoints as they have always been company’s most vulnerable assets to attacks, regardless of where they are located inside or outside the corporate network, exploiting multiple factors such as human errors, device misconfigurations, software breaches, weak passwords, missing patches, and many more. Munisys’ Endpoint Security solutions offer comprehensive monitoring and protection features to desktops, laptops, smartphones, printers, and other connected user devices. Advanced capabilities are also leveraged to provide higher levels of protection like anomaly behavior analysis, automatic enforcement, and dynamic security controls. We value also built-in hardware-based security and advanced self-healing capabilities as integrated features supported by specific endpoint devices being part of our EndPoint Solutions Business Unit’ catalog.
